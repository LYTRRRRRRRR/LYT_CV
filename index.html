Strategic Queuing Disclosure under Service Quality Uncertainty with Value-at-Risk Preferences

第二作者2nd Author 2025 – 2026 European Journal of Operational Research (SCI-E) 在审Under review at EJOR (SCI-E)
  • 模型构建：构建贝叶斯说服博弈模型——顾客到达服从泊松过程、服务时间服从指数分布、服务质量高低不确定；顾客具有基于 Value-at-Risk 的风险偏好与等待时间认知偏差（有限理性），服务商承诺队列长度披露策略，顾客据此更新信念并做出加入/退出决策。 Model: Bayesian persuasion game where arrivals follow a Poisson process, service times are exponential, and service quality is uncertain; customers have Value-at-Risk preferences and waiting-time cognitive biases (bounded rationality). The provider commits to a queue-length disclosure policy; customers update beliefs and decide to join or balk.
  • 方法运用：贝叶斯说服框架 + 几何凸组合方法刻画最优披露策略；排队论与 VaR 效用函数推导顾客均衡策略；逆向归纳与数值分析求解利润最大化及社会福利最大化下的最优披露策略。 Methods: Bayesian persuasion framework with geometric convex combination to characterize optimal disclosure; queueing theory and VaR utility for customer equilibrium; backward induction and numerical analysis for profit- and welfare-maximizing policies.
  • 核心发现：发现"过早退出"现象——风险厌恶顾客即使预期效用为正也可能因规避尾部风险而退出；最优披露策略随需求从完全隐藏过渡到混合披露再到完全透明；认知偏差具有双重效应；极端风险厌恶下存在"心理失效区"，信息设计完全无效。 Findings: Identify "early balking" — risk-averse customers may quit despite positive expected utility to avoid tail risk. Optimal disclosure shifts from full concealment to partial to full transparency as demand grows; cognitive biases have dual effects; under extreme risk aversion a "psychological failure zone" renders information design ineffective.

组合数学构造问题 · 集合族交集极值问题Combinatorial Construction · Extremal Set Intersection Problem

独立研究Independent Research2025 – 2026
  • 研究问题：在 n 元集合族中，任意两集合交集恰为 1 且任意元素恰出现 n 次时，求集合数与元素数及其构造方法。 Problem: For a finite set family in which any two sets intersect in exactly one element and each element belongs to exactly n sets, determine the numbers of sets and elements, and give a construction.
  • 核心结论：最大集合数与元素数相等，均为 n(n−1)+1。 Result: The maximum number of sets equals the number of elements, both being n(n−1)+1.
  • 构造方法：元素视为点、集合视为直线，把集合相交问题转化为二维平面的直线相交问题。 Construction: View elements as points and sets as lines, reducing set intersection to line intersection in the plane.

是否公开质量？在"限量供应"销售模式下的产品质量披露策略To Disclose Quality or Not? Product Quality Disclosure under Limited-Supply Sales

第一作者1st Author2025已录用Accepted
  • 研究问题：双寡头竞争市场中，高、低质量企业如何在"限量供应"模式下制定质量披露与产量策略，以传递质量信号并实现利润最大化。 Problem: In a duopoly, how do high- and low-quality firms set quality-disclosure and output strategies under limited supply to signal quality and maximize profit?
  • 模型与方法：两阶段动态博弈模型 + 库诺特竞争 + 精炼贝叶斯均衡框架，逆向归纳求解分离均衡与最优定产策略。 Model & Methods: Two-stage dynamic game with Cournot competition, solved for perfect Bayesian equilibrium; backward induction yields separating equilibria and optimal output strategies.
  • 核心发现：存在低产与高产两种分离均衡，成本结构决定均衡选择；高成本企业倾向主动披露质量，低成本企业选择不披露，披露与定产策略需协同优化。 Findings: Two separating equilibria (low/high output) exist, selected by cost structure; high-cost firms disclose quality while low-cost firms stay silent; disclosure and output must be jointly optimized.
  • 个人贡献：独立完成模型推导、均衡求解、数值仿真与论文撰写。 Contribution: Independently completed model derivation, equilibrium solving, numerical simulation, and paper writing.

基于改进 1DCNN 模型的二氧化碳排放量统计监测Statistical Monitoring of CO₂ Emissions via Improved 1DCNN

项目负责人Project Leader 2023.04 – 2023.06 第九届全国大学生统计建模大赛9th National Statistical Modeling Contest for College Students全国三等奖National Third Prize

本项目使用统计建模大赛官方数据库与世界银行数据库，整合 1990–2021 年的二氧化碳排放量、经济发展、人口规模、消费水平、能源生产与消费相关指标，建立面板数据。从时序性、相关性的角度，分析影响二氧化碳排放量的多种因素及其影响程度。结合实际分析后，发现选取的指标均与二氧化碳排放密切相关，可以作为深度学习模型的输入变量。 Using the competition's official database and the World Bank database, this project integrated CO₂ emissions, economic development, population, consumption, and energy indicators (1990–2021) into panel data, analyzing the drivers of emissions from time-series and correlation perspectives. All selected indicators proved closely related to emissions and suitable as deep-learning inputs.

根据数据特征，选用一维卷积神经网络（1DCNN）、长短期记忆网络（LSTM）与残差网络（ResNet）进行对比实验，根据对比结果与特点，针对性地进行网络融合与架构改进，建立了 CNN-RESNET、CNN-LSTM 与改进的 1DCNN 模型。其中改进 1DCNN 模型的 RMSE 值为 55.290、MAE 值为 36.730，年度碳排放量监测值与真实值拟合度最高、曲线误差最小，训练效果远高于其余模型，最终选用其作为创新模型。 Based on data characteristics, 1DCNN, LSTM, and ResNet were compared, then fused and improved into CNN-RESNET, CNN-LSTM, and an improved 1DCNN. The improved 1DCNN achieved RMSE 55.290 and MAE 36.730 with the best fit and smallest curve error — far outperforming the others — and was adopted as the final model.

本文综合考量多维指标的影响，提出了一种效率高、成本低、精度高、可推广的改进 1DCNN 模型，为碳排放监测问题提供了可参考的新方法，从及时监测的前提条件入手，对"双碳"目标的加快实现具有重要意义。 Overall, the proposed model is efficient, low-cost, accurate, and generalizable, offering a new reference method for emission monitoring and accelerating China's dual-carbon goals through timely measurement.

Z 世代大学生社交媒体潮流爱好信息采纳行为影响因素研究Drivers of Gen-Z Information Adoption for Social-Media Trendy Hobbies

国家级创新训练项目 | 项目负责人National Innovation Training Program | Project Leader 2022.06 – 2023.06 论文第一作者刊录《时代经贸》First-author paper published in Times Economy & Trade
